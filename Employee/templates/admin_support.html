  Frequently Asked Questions

How can I manage employees?

Use the Employee Management section from the Admin Dashboard to add, edit and manage employee records.

Where can I manage attendance?

Open Attendance Management from the dashboard to mark, update and view employee attendance.

Where can I manage salaries?

The Salary Management section allows the administrator to manage employee salary records.

How can I approve leave requests?

Open Leave Management to view pending requests and approve or reject employee leaves.
